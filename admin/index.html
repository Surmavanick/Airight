In hosted mode, image bytes are sent to AI or Not through Aright's server-side proxy; the vendor states uploads are deleted after inference and its privacy policy applies. Local-only mode keeps the image on this computer, while hybrid local mode shows both named signals.

Aright extracts evenly spaced frames in your browser and sends only those frames to the connected image detector; the full video is not uploaded. This is frame-level screening, not full temporal deepfake analysis.

The hosted console sends a 16 kHz voice sample to AI or Not; the local app uses Spectra-AASIST3. Both screen synthetic or cloned speech, not AI music. A supplied script is checked separately.
